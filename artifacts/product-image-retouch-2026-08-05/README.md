# Product image retouch — 2026-08-05

Updated exactly five dessert catalog images: Napoleon, chocolate muffin, Spanish cheesecake, Tary cheesecake, and meringue roll. The two cheesecake images were mirrored horizontally. High-resolution source photographs replaced the low-resolution Napoleon and muffin crops. White background, scale, restrained color correction, edge cleanup, and WebP/sRGB export were applied locally. No external service or generative redraw was used.

`originals/` contains the exact replaced files, `final-assets/` contains the delivered versions, and `provenance.json` records SHA-256 checksums and final target paths.
